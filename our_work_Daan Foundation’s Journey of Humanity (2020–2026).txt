Daan Foundation’s Journey of Humanity (2020–2026)

Daan Foundation began with a small humanitarian effort in 2020, when a few volunteers decided to distribute food during Ramadan for only 15 days — from the 15th Roza until the 30th Roza before Eid.

At that time, it was not planned as a long-term organization or a large charitable project. The intention was simple: to help people who were struggling for food during difficult times.

However, what started as a temporary Ramadan initiative gradually transformed into a continuous humanitarian mission.

During the pandemic and lockdown period, many families faced unemployment, hunger, and financial hardship. While distributing food, the volunteers realized that the need for support would not end after Eid. Because of this, the work continued even after Ramadan ended.

Over the years, Daan Foundation steadily expanded its activities with the support of donors, volunteers, and community members who believed in helping humanity through compassion and charity.



2020 — The Beginning of a Mission

In 2020, Daan Foundation started its first food distribution effort during Ramadan.

The original plan was to provide cooked meals only from the 15th Roza until the 30th Roza — a total of 15 days.

Despite limited resources, volunteers worked continuously to prepare and distribute meals to people affected by poverty and lockdown conditions.

That small initiative became the starting point of a much larger humanitarian journey.



2021 — Expanding Food & Iftar Support

As awareness about the foundation’s work increased, more people came forward to support the mission.

During Ramadan 2021, Daan Foundation expanded its Iftar arrangements and started reaching larger numbers of fasting individuals and struggling families.

The organization also began distributing ration kits containing essential food supplies to households facing financial difficulties.

At the same time, the Community Kitchen initiative became more organized and started providing regular cooked meals to vulnerable people.



2022 — Building a Stronger Community Kitchen

By 2022, the Community Kitchen had become one of the foundation’s most important humanitarian initiatives.

Hundreds of meals were being prepared and distributed regularly to laborers, elderly individuals, patients, travelers, and poor families.

The organization also expanded its welfare activities by:

distributing clothes to needy families,

helping financially struggling families during marriages,

and supporting patients with medical treatment assistance.

Daan Foundation’s work was no longer limited to Ramadan. It had become a year-round mission of service.



2023 — Reaching More People in Need

In 2023, Daan Foundation continued to grow with the support of donors and volunteers.

Food distribution activities expanded further, and more ration kits were delivered to families facing hardship.

The foundation also strengthened its emergency support efforts and continued helping vulnerable communities during difficult situations.

Every year, the number of people benefiting from the foundation’s support continued increasing.



2024 — Strengthening Humanitarian Efforts

By 2024, Daan Foundation had become a trusted community-driven charitable initiative for many vulnerable families.

Its daily food distribution and Ramadan Iftar programs expanded significantly, while welfare support activities continued helping people in need with dignity and compassion.

The organization also improved its ability to organize charitable campaigns and community support programs more effectively.



2025 — Thousands Benefiting During Ramadan

What once started with helping around 1,000 people during Ramadan had now expanded dramatically.

During Ramadan 2025, Daan Foundation was able to provide Iftar support to thousands of fasting individuals through cooked meals, food distribution, and ration assistance programs.

The Community Kitchen continued serving hundreds of meals daily through the support of:

Zakat,

Sadaqah,

charity donations,

and community contributions.

The scale of humanitarian work had grown far beyond what anyone imagined in 2020.



2026 — Continuing the Journey of Humanity

By 2026, Daan Foundation had distributed more than 500,000 food plates and meals through its various humanitarian activities.

From a small 15-day Ramadan effort to a growing charitable foundation, the journey reflects the power of sincere intentions, community support, and consistent humanitarian work.

Today, the foundation continues serving vulnerable communities through:

Daily food distribution

Community Kitchen services

Ramadan Iftar arrangements

Ration kit distribution

Clothing support for needy families

Medical assistance for patients

Welfare and emergency support programs



A Mission Powered by Compassion

Every meal distributed, every ration kit delivered, and every family supported represents the generosity of donors, volunteers, and supporters who believe in helping humanity.

Daan Foundation continues its journey with the same spirit with which it began in 2020:serving people with dignity, compassion, responsibility, and hope.

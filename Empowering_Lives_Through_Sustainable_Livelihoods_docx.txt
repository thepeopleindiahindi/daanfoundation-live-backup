Empowering Lives Through Sustainable Livelihoods

Building Self-Reliance Across Communities in India

At Daan Foundation, we believe that true charity goes beyond providing immediate relief — it also means helping people stand on their own feet. While food support remains our core mission, we are equally committed to creating opportunities that enable individuals and families to earn a dignified and sustainable livelihood.

Across India, many hardworking individuals are willing to work but are unable to move forward due to a lack of financial support, resources, or guidance. This gap often keeps families trapped in poverty despite their willingness to improve their lives.

Daan Foundation is dedicated to bridging this gap.



From Support to Self-Reliance

Our approach is simple:

👉 Identify individuals who are willing to work👉 Understand their skills or interests👉 Provide small but meaningful support👉 Help them become self-reliant

We focus on practical, small-scale livelihood support that can immediately generate income and stability.



Real-Life Livelihood Support Initiatives

Daan Foundation has supported many individuals through small but impactful livelihood opportunities, such as:

🔹 Small Business Support

We help individuals start or strengthen small businesses by providing initial resources like:

Grocery items for a general store

Basic setup support for street vending

Small capital assistance for daily trade

🔹 Ice Cream & Street Cart Setup

For individuals with limited income options, setting up a small ice cream cart or food cart can become a stable source of daily earnings.Daan Foundation helps by arranging basic setup and initial stock support.



🔹 Sewing Machines for Women

Many women, especially housewives and widows, have stitching skills but lack the tools to earn.

We provide:

Sewing machines

Basic support to start home-based tailoring work

This allows women to:

Earn from home

Support their families

Maintain dignity and independence



🔹 Skill-Based Support

In cases where individuals require skill development, we also try to:

Connect them with basic training opportunities

Encourage skill-building for long-term growth



Stories of Change

“From Daily Struggle to Daily Income”

Rafiq, a daily wage worker, often struggled to find consistent work. With small support from Daan Foundation, he started a street cart business. Today, he earns regularly and supports his family with dignity.



“A Sewing Machine Changed My Life”

Shabana, a homemaker, wanted to contribute financially but had no means. After receiving a sewing machine, she started stitching clothes from home and now earns enough to support her children’s education.



“A Small Shop, A Big Change”

Imran received help to add stock to his small general store. With improved inventory, his sales increased, helping him stabilize his household income.

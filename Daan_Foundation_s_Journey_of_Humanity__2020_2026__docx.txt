Daan Foundation’s Journey of Humanity (2020–2026)

From a Small Iftar Initiative to a Growing Mission of Service

Daan Foundation was born in India during the blessed month of Ramadan in 2020 with a simple but powerful mission — no hungry person should sleep without food. What began as a small local iftar distribution effort supported by only a few well-wishers has today grown into a trusted humanitarian initiative serving thousands of people across different communities in India.

In the early days, there was no large office, no complex infrastructure, and no international funding. There were only sincere intentions, community trust, and a handful of people determined to help those struggling with hunger and poverty. Some supporters contributed simply by believing in our honesty and dedication. They encouraged us to continue even when resources were limited.

By the grace of Allah and the support of donors, volunteers, and ordinary families, that small initiative slowly transformed into a wider movement of compassion and dignity.



Our Mission

Daan Foundation works only within India and focuses on serving vulnerable communities through food support, iftar distribution, ration assistance, clothing aid, education support, medical assistance, livelihood help, and humanitarian care.

Our primary mission is simple:

“No person should remain hungry when help is possible.”

Every day, we try to support people who are struggling due to poverty, illness, unemployment, disability, old age, or financial hardship.

We believe charity is not only about giving food — it is about restoring dignity, hope, and human connection.



Community Kitchen: Feeding With Dignity

One of the most important initiatives of Daan Foundation is our Community Kitchen.

Every evening, meals are prepared and distributed to people from all religions, castes, and backgrounds without discrimination. The kitchen serves labourers, widows, elderly individuals, disabled persons, daily wage workers, students, and families facing financial difficulties.

Since 2020, Daan Foundation has distributed more than 500,000+ meals and food packets across various humanitarian campaigns and community food drives.

The estimated cost of serving one meal currently averages around ₹59 per person, including preparation, packaging, transportation, and distribution.

Different meals are prepared regularly to ensure dignity and nutrition, including rice dishes, dal, vegetables, roti, khichdi, seasonal meals, and special Ramadan iftar arrangements.

For many people, this evening meal becomes the most important meal of their day.



Ramadan: Where Our Journey Began

Ramadan remains at the heart of Daan Foundation’s identity.

The foundation itself began through iftar distribution during Ramadan 2020, when a few volunteers noticed that many fasting individuals, labourers, widows, and poor families were struggling even to arrange basic iftar meals.

What started as a pilot effort to provide iftar to a few hundred people gradually expanded year after year.

In the beginning, around 1,000 people benefitted from the initiative. By Ramadan 2025, Daan Foundation’s iftar and food support initiatives had expanded to thousands of beneficiaries across different areas.

Supporters from across India contributed through zakat, sadaqah, fidya, kaffarah, fitrana, ration donations, and direct food sponsorships.



Serving Every Needy Person With Dignity

Daan Foundation believes humanitarian service should reach everyone with respect and compassion.

We try to ensure that widows, elderly women, housewives facing financial hardship, pregnant women, orphaned children, disabled individuals, and struggling families receive support without humiliation or discrimination.

Our volunteers regularly meet people personally, understand their conditions, and try to help within available resources.

Whether it is providing cooked meals, ration kits, educational support, medical help, or seasonal assistance, the aim remains the same:

“Every human being deserves dignity.”



Supporting Women and Families

Daan Foundation is committed to helping vulnerable women and families facing financial hardship.

Many women silently carry the burden of feeding families while managing homes during difficult circumstances. Some are widows, some are abandoned, and many belong to low-income households where basic food itself becomes uncertain.

Through food assistance, ration support, Ramadan meal drives, emergency help, and educational support for children, Daan Foundation tries to reduce some of these burdens.

The organisation also believes that women empowerment begins with ensuring access to basic necessities like food, dignity, healthcare, and education.

Transparency and Trust

From the beginning, Daan Foundation has operated on community trust.

Many early supporters contributed even before the organisation had a formal online presence because they personally witnessed the sincerity and dedication of volunteers.

The foundation believes every donation is an amanah — a trust that must be used responsibly and honestly.

Whether a person contributes ₹100 or sponsors large-scale food distribution, every contribution helps someone facing hardship.

Looking Ahead

A Shared Journey of Humanity

Daan Foundation believes real change happens when ordinary people come together to help others sincerely.

Every donor, volunteer, supporter, cook, driver, and helper has played a role in this journey.

The foundation may have started small, but the compassion behind it continues to grow every year.

And as long as there are people struggling with hunger, hardship, and poverty, Daan Foundation will continue trying — one meal, one family, and one act of kindness at a time.

What is Sadaqah?

Daan Foundation believes that Sadaqah is one of the most beautiful forms of compassion and humanity in Islam.

Sadaqah means voluntary charity given sincerely for the sake of Allah (SWT). It can be:

financial support,

feeding hungry people,

helping poor families,

supporting patients,

providing clothes,

helping someone in difficulty,

or even simple acts of kindness such as a smile, respect, or helping hand.

Unlike Zakat, Sadaqah is not compulsory. A person can give any amount at any time according to their ability and intention.



The Meaning of Sadaqah

The word Sadaqah comes from the Arabic word:

“Sidq” — meaning sincerity

This reflects that true charity comes from a sincere heart and genuine concern for others.

In Islam, helping people with kindness and compassion is considered a noble and rewarding act.



Sadaqah in the Light of Islam

Allah (SWT) says in the Qur’an:

“Who is it that would loan Allah a goodly loan so He may multiply it for him many times over?”(Qur’an 2:245)

The Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him) said:

“Sadaqah extinguishes sin as water extinguishes fire.”(Hadith – Tirmidhi)

He (PBUH) was also known for increasing charity during Ramadan and helping poor and needy people generously.



How Daan Foundation Uses Sadaqah

Since 2020, Daan Foundation has been utilizing Sadaqah, charity contributions, and community support to help vulnerable and needy people within India.

The organization works only within India and focuses on humanitarian support for struggling communities through:

Community Kitchen services,

daily food distribution,

Ramadan Iftar arrangements,

ration kit support,

clothing distribution,

medical assistance,

educational support,

marriage assistance for poor families,

and small livelihood support efforts.

The foundation’s primary humanitarian mission remains:

to help ensure that no needy person sleeps hungry.



Community Kitchen & Daily Food Support

One of the foundation’s most important humanitarian initiatives is its Community Kitchen, where fresh cooked meals are distributed daily in the evening to people in need.

Since 2020, the foundation has distributed:

500,000+ Meals

to laborers, poor families, elderly individuals, travelers, widows, patients, and financially struggling people.

Food is distributed without discrimination based on:

religion,

caste,

language,

or social background.

The organization believes hunger affects humanity equally, and support should be provided with dignity and compassion.



Sadaqah Jariyah — Ongoing Charity

Islam also teaches the concept of:

Sadaqah Jariyah (Continuous Charity)

This refers to charity that continues benefiting people over time.

Examples may include:

feeding people regularly,

supporting education,

helping someone become self-dependent,

or contributing toward long-term humanitarian work.

Daan Foundation believes that helping communities consistently creates long-term positive impact and hope for vulnerable families.



More Than Food Support

While food assistance remains the foundation’s central focus, Daan Foundation also tries to support people in other humanitarian areas whenever possible.

These efforts include:

helping poor children continue education,

supporting patients with medical assistance,

distributing clothes to needy individuals,

helping poor families during marriages,

especially assisting girls from financially struggling households,

and encouraging small livelihood opportunities to help people stand on their own feet.

The foundation believes that compassion should not stop at emergency help alone.



Why Sadaqah Matters

Sadaqah not only helps poor and needy people — it also strengthens humanity, compassion, and unity within society.

Even a small act of charity can:

reduce someone’s suffering,

provide hope,

restore dignity,

and bring comfort during difficult times.

The foundation believes every contribution, whether large or small, has the power to make a meaningful difference in someone’s life.



Transparency & Responsibility

Daan Foundation believes every donation is an amanah — a trust.

The organization remains committed to:

honesty,

responsible use of donations,

transparency,

and humanitarian service with dignity.

Operational support is necessary to continue food preparation, transportation, volunteer coordination, and welfare activities effectively and consistently.

The foundation believes trust is built through sincere service and responsible humanitarian work.



Working Only Within India

Daan Foundation operates only within India and focuses entirely on helping communities within Indian geography.

The organization:

does not operate internationally,

does not conduct activities outside India,

and is not authorized to receive donations from outside India.

Its mission is centered on serving vulnerable people locally through compassion, responsibility, and community support.



A Mission of Humanity & Compassion

Every meal distributed, every family supported, and every act of kindness represents the compassion of people who believe in helping humanity sincerely.

Daan Foundation continues its humanitarian journey with the belief that:

Sadaqah is not only charity — it is kindness, dignity, hope, and humanity in action.

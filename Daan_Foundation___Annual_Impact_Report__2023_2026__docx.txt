Daan Foundation – Annual Impact Report (2023–2026)

Serving Humanity Through Food, Dignity & Compassion (India)



About Daan Foundation

Daan Foundation is a humanitarian initiative committed to one clear and powerful mission:

“No one should sleep hungry.”

Founded in 2020 as a small community effort to provide food during Ramadan, the Foundation has grown steadily through trust, dedication, and community support. In 2023, Daan Foundation was formally registered, strengthening its commitment to structured, transparent, and accountable charitable work.

Since its inception, the Foundation has been working continuously across India, focusing primarily on feeding the hungry while also supporting communities through other humanitarian efforts such as clothing distribution, basic medical support, and livelihood assistance.

Important Note:Daan Foundation operates strictly within India and does not conduct activities or accept donations from outside India.



Our Core Mission

The primary objective of Daan Foundation is:

To provide daily meals to those in need

To ensure no individual remains hungry

To serve all communities equally, without discrimination based on religion, caste, or background

Every person — regardless of identity — can access food through our initiatives with dignity and respect.



Community Kitchen – Daily Food Support

At the heart of our work lies the Community Kitchen, which operates daily and provides cooked meals to individuals in need.

Key Highlights:

Daily evening meal distribution

Open to all communities without discrimination

Nutritious and freshly prepared food

Average cost per meal: approximately ₹59

For many beneficiaries, this meal is the only reliable food they receive in a day.



Ramadan Food & Iftar Programme

Ramadan holds special importance in the journey of Daan Foundation. What began as a small iftar distribution effort in 2020 has now become a large-scale seasonal initiative.

Impact During Ramadan (2023–2026):

Thousands of individuals served every year

Identification of vulnerable fasting individuals

Daily Iftar meal distribution

In special cases, Sehri (pre-dawn meal) support

The Foundation prioritizes:

Daily wage workers

Elderly individuals

Widows

Families unable to arrange iftar

This ensures that even those fasting under hardship can break their fast with dignity.

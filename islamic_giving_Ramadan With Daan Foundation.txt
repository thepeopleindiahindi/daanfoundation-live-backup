Ramadan With Daan Foundation

Daan Foundation was born during the blessed month of Ramadan in 2020 through a simple humanitarian effort:

providing Iftar meals to people in need.

At that time, the initiative was not planned as a large organization or long-term project. It began as a small community effort intended to run only from the 15th fast of Ramadan until Eid — approximately 15 days.

A few people came together with the intention of helping needy individuals who struggled to arrange food for Iftar during Ramadan.

What started as a temporary relief effort gradually became a continuous humanitarian mission that still continues today.



A Journey Built on Compassion & Trust

In the early days, Daan Foundation had:

no official office,

no formal structure,

no website,

and very limited resources.

Yet many people trusted the mission and supported the effort simply by seeing the sincerity and humanitarian intention behind the work.

Local supporters, volunteers, and well-wishers encouraged the continuation of the project and believed that feeding hungry people was a noble and necessary effort.

The foundation believes this journey has continued not only because of human effort, but through the mercy and help of Allah (SWT), along with the support and trust of compassionate people.



Ramadan — A Month of Compassion

Ramadan is a sacred and deeply spiritual month for Muslims around the world.

It is a month of:

fasting,

prayer,

patience,

charity,

compassion,

and helping those in need.

While many families break their fasts peacefully, countless vulnerable individuals struggle to arrange even a single meal for Iftar.

This is where humanitarian support becomes important.



Iftar Distribution Since 2020

Since its beginning, Daan Foundation has continued organizing:

Iftar Meal Distribution

during Ramadan for:

laborers,

poor families,

travelers,

elderly people,

patients,

widows,

and financially struggling individuals.

What began with support for around:

1,000 people during the early Ramadan efforts

has gradually expanded over the years.

By Ramadan 2025, the foundation was helping provide Iftar support and meals to thousands of people through its Community Kitchen and Ramadan initiatives.



Community Kitchen — Continuing Beyond Ramadan

One of the most remarkable parts of this journey is that the work did not stop after Ramadan ended.

The original Iftar initiative gradually transformed into a:

Daily Community Kitchen

that continues serving free evening meals throughout the year.

Since 2020, the foundation has distributed:

500,000+ Meals

to people facing hunger and financial hardship within India.

This humanitarian effort continues daily with the support of donors, volunteers, and compassionate community members.



Food Without Discrimination

Daan Foundation believes:

hunger has no religion, caste, or social status.

Meals are provided respectfully to anyone in need without discrimination based on:

religion,

caste,

language,

or community background.

The organization’s goal is simple:

that no needy person should sleep hungry.



The Spirit of Charity in Ramadan

Ramadan is also a month in which Muslims increase:

Zakat,

Sadaqah,

charity,

compassion,

and humanitarian support.

The Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him) was known for increasing generosity during Ramadan and helping needy people abundantly.

Through community support, Daan Foundation tries to continue this spirit of compassion by helping vulnerable people with:

Iftar meals,

food support,

and humanitarian assistance.



More Than Food Support

While feeding hungry people remains its central humanitarian mission, Daan Foundation also supports communities through:

clothing distribution,

educational assistance,

medical support,

marriage assistance for poor families,

especially support for girls from financially struggling households,

and small livelihood support efforts.

The organization believes helping humanity means standing beside people during difficult moments of life.

Working Only Within India

Daan Foundation operates only within India and focuses entirely on charitable and humanitarian activities within Indian geography.

The organization:

does not operate internationally,

does not conduct activities outside India,

and is not authorized to receive donations from outside India.

Its humanitarian mission is dedicated to serving local communities with compassion, dignity, and responsibility.

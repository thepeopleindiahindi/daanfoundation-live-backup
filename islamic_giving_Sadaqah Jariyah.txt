What is Sadaqah Jariyah?

Daan Foundation believes that Sadaqah Jariyah is one of the most meaningful and rewarding forms of charity in Islam.

Sadaqah Jariyah means:

“ongoing charity”

It refers to any charitable act that continues benefiting people over time, even after the donor has passed away.

In Islam, this form of charity is considered extremely valuable because its reward continues as long as people continue benefiting from it.



Sadaqah Jariyah in Islam

The Prophet Muhammad (Peace Be Upon Him) said:

“When a person dies, all their deeds end except three: a continuing charity, beneficial knowledge, and a righteous child who prays for them.”(Hadith – Sahih Muslim)

This is why Muslims often support long-term humanitarian and welfare initiatives that continue helping communities for years.



Why Sadaqah Jariyah Is Important

Sadaqah Jariyah is not only about donating money.

It is about creating lasting benefit for humanity.

A small act done sincerely can continue helping:

poor families,

hungry people,

children,

patients,

travelers,

and struggling communities for a long time.

The reward of such charity continues as long as people continue benefiting from it.



Sadaqah Jariyah at Daan Foundation

Since 2020, Daan Foundation has been working within India to help vulnerable communities through continuous humanitarian efforts.

The organization works only within India and focuses entirely on local charitable and welfare activities.

The foundation believes that regular food support and community welfare initiatives are among the most meaningful forms of ongoing charity because they continuously help people facing hardship.



Community Kitchen — An Ongoing Charity

One of the foundation’s most important humanitarian initiatives is its:

Community Kitchen

What started during Ramadan 2020 as a small 15-day food initiative gradually became a continuous humanitarian effort serving people daily.

Every evening, freshly prepared meals are distributed to:

laborers,

poor families,

elderly individuals,

widows,

travelers,

patients,

and financially struggling people.

Since 2020, Daan Foundation has distributed:

500,000+ Meals

through its Community Kitchen and food support programs.

The organization believes that feeding hungry people regularly is among the most impactful forms of Sadaqah Jariyah.



Serving Humanity Without Discrimination

Food support and humanitarian assistance are provided without discrimination based on:

religion,

caste,

language,

community,

or background.

Anyone in need can receive food respectfully and with dignity.

The foundation believes:

hunger has no religion, and compassion should have no boundaries.



Examples of Sadaqah Jariyah

In Islam, many acts can become Sadaqah Jariyah when they continue benefiting people.

Examples include:

feeding people regularly,

helping educate a child,

supporting medical care,

helping someone become self-dependent,

distributing useful knowledge,

planting trees,

supporting community welfare initiatives,

or helping sustain humanitarian services.

Even small consistent acts of kindness can become ongoing charity.



More Than Just Food Support

While food distribution remains its central humanitarian mission, Daan Foundation also tries to support communities through:

ration kit distribution,

clothing support,

educational assistance,

medical help for needy patients,

marriage assistance for poor families,

especially support for girls from financially struggling households,

and small livelihood support efforts.

The organization believes that helping people regain dignity and stability creates long-term positive impact within communities.



A Charity That Continues Helping

Daan Foundation believes true humanitarian work is not limited to temporary relief alone.

Continuous support can:

reduce suffering,

strengthen communities,

provide hope,

and inspire kindness among people.

This is why ongoing humanitarian initiatives are so important.

Even a single contribution toward feeding hungry people can become a source of continuous reward and relief.



Transparency & Responsibility

The foundation considers every donation an amanah — a trust.

Daan Foundation remains committed to:

honesty,

responsible use of donations,

transparency,

and humanitarian service with dignity.

Operational support is necessary to continue food preparation, transportation, volunteer coordination, and welfare activities effectively.

The organization believes trust is built through sincerity, accountability, and consistent service to humanity.



Working Only Within India

Daan Foundation operates only within India and focuses entirely on helping communities within Indian geography.

The organization:

does not operate internationally,

does not conduct activities outside India,

and is not authorized to receive donations from outside India.

Its humanitarian mission is dedicated to serving vulnerable communities locally with compassion and responsibility.



A Mission of Ongoing Compassion

Every meal served, every family supported, and every act of kindness reflects the spirit of Sadaqah Jariyah.

Daan Foundation continues its journey with the belief that:

continuous charity has the power to bring lasting hope, dignity, and relief to humanity.

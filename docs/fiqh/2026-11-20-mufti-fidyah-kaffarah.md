# Mufti sahab ka jawab: Fidyah aur Kaffarah (20 Nov 2026)

## Jawab (sirf ye 4 baatein)

1. Qasam ka kaffara = 10 miskeenon ko 2 waqt pet bhar khana.
2. Ek roze ka fidya = sadaqa-e-fitr ke barabar, yani paune do kilo gehun ya uski qeemat.
3. "Ek iftar + ek khana" fidya NAHI hai.
4. Daan Foundation sirf 1 waqt khana deti hai, isliye khane ke zariye kaffara nahi liya ja sakta.

## Niyam

- Is note me apni taraf se koi naya mas'ala nahi joda gaya hai.
- Jo baat in 4 baaton me nahi hai (jaise roze ka kaffara/60 miskeen, kapde dena, ration kit, gehun ki aaj ki qeemat rupaye me), wo "Mufti sahab se poochhna hai" hai, tay nahi.

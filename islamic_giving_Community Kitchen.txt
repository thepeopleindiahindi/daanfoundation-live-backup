Community Kitchen

Serving Free Meals With Dignity & Compassion

Daan Foundation has been operating its Community Kitchen continuously since 2020 with one simple mission:

to ensure that no needy person sleeps hungry.

What started during Ramadan in 2020 as a small food distribution effort for only 15 days gradually transformed into a regular humanitarian initiative that now serves free meals daily to people in need.

Every evening, the Community Kitchen prepares and distributes fresh cooked food to vulnerable individuals and families without discrimination based on religion, caste, background, or social status.

Anyone in need can come and receive food with dignity and respect.



A Daily Mission Against Hunger

Hunger is one of the most painful realities faced by poor and struggling communities. For many daily wage workers, laborers, elderly individuals, patients, travelers, widows, and financially struggling families, arranging even one proper meal can become difficult.

Daan Foundation’s Community Kitchen works every day to reduce this hardship by providing nutritious evening meals to those who need support.

The kitchen currently distributes food daily, and since 2020, the foundation has already served approximately:

500,000+ Free Meals

to people facing food insecurity and hardship across local communities in India.

This journey has only been possible because of the support of donors, volunteers, and local communities who believe in helping humanity through compassion and charity.



Food Served With Respect

One of the most important principles of the Community Kitchen is dignity.

Every person who comes for food is treated respectfully, regardless of:

religion,

caste,

community,

language,

or financial background.

The foundation believes hunger does not discriminate between people — and humanitarian support should not discriminate either.

Every evening, people from different walks of life come to receive meals peacefully and respectfully.



Fresh Meals Prepared Daily

The Community Kitchen provides different types of freshly prepared meals regularly so that people receive nutritious and satisfying food.

Depending on available resources and support, meals may include:

rice,

dal,

vegetables,

roti,

khichdi,

seasonal food items,

and other freshly cooked meals.

Food is prepared carefully by volunteers and kitchen helpers to ensure cleanliness, quality, and proper distribution.

Currently, the estimated cost of providing one meal is approximately:

₹59 Per Person

Even a small contribution can help feed someone who may otherwise sleep hungry.

A Vision for the Future

Daan Foundation hopes to expand the Community Kitchen initiative further and reach more people across different regions within India.

The long-term vision is to establish more community-based food support systems so that vulnerable individuals in more areas can access free meals with dignity.

The organization believes that collective compassion can help build a society where no person sleeps hungry.

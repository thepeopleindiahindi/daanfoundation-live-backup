A Message from Daan Foundation

At Daan Foundation, every initiative we undertake is rooted in one simple belief: acts of faith should be easy, transparent, and accessible to everyone. Over the years, as our community has grown, so has the number of requests we receive from donors wishing to fulfil their religious obligations — Qurbani, Aqeeqa, and Sadqa — through a platform they can trust completely.

To serve this need with the focus and dedication it deserves, Daan Foundation is proud to formally introduce Qurbani Easy (qurbanieasy.com), our sister concern platform built specifically to handle all sacrifice-related religious services. This announcement is both an update for our existing donor community and an invitation to new families who wish to fulfil their religious duties with confidence, ease, and full accountability.

Why Qurbani Easy Was Created

Religious sacrifice — whether it is Qurbani, Aqeeqa, or Sadqa — carries deep spiritual weight for every Muslim family. It is not simply a transaction; it is an act of obedience, gratitude, and charity that connects the giver to countless people in need across India and beyond. Given the sensitivity and significance of this responsibility, Daan Foundation felt it was important to build a platform solely dedicated to these services, rather than folding them into our broader donation categories.

Qurbani Easy was therefore created as a specialised, purpose-built website under the Daan Foundation umbrella. While Daan Foundation continues to handle a wide range of charitable causes — education support, ration kits, community kitchens, and general Sadqa/Zakat donations — Qurbani Easy focuses exclusively on sacrifice-based religious duties. This separation allows us to give each area of our work the attention, process design, and reporting rigour it truly deserves.

Qurbani Easy operates under the same trust, the same governance, and the same bank account as Daan Foundation. In other words, when you give through Qurbani Easy, you are giving directly to Daan Foundation — the same registered public charitable trust our donors have relied on for years. The only difference is the platform is designed and organised specifically around the rituals of Qurbani, Aqeeqa, and Sadqa.

What You Can Do on Qurbani Easy

Qurbani Easy currently offers donors a complete, easy-to-navigate set of services for every type of sacrifice-related obligation:

1. Qurbani (Udhiyah)

Book your Qurbani for Eid-ul-Adha with complete transparency. From selection of the animal share to the distribution of meat among those in need, every step is designed to give donors peace of mind that their religious obligation has been fulfilled correctly and on time.

2. Aqeeqa

Celebrate the birth of a child according to Sunnah by arranging Aqeeqa through Qurbani Easy. Families across India and abroad can now fulfil this beautiful tradition without having to coordinate logistics themselves — Qurbani Easy handles the entire process on their behalf.

3. Sadqa Qurbani

Not all sacrifice-based giving is tied to Eid. Many donors wish to give Sadqa Qurbani throughout the year — whether for the fulfilment of a vow (Mannat), as an act of repentance, or simply as ongoing charity. Qurbani Easy makes it possible to give Sadqa Qurbani at any time of the year, not just during the Eid season.

4. Eid-ul-Adha Qurbani Packages

During the Qurbani season, Qurbani Easy offers structured packages that let donors choose their preferred share size, distribution preference, and location. Every package is built to maximise both the spiritual reward for the donor and the real-world impact for the recipients.

5. Ramzan & Eid-Related Charitable Packages

Beyond Qurbani season, Qurbani Easy also supports Ramzan-related giving — from Iftar contributions to Sadqa-tul-Fitr — ensuring that our donor community has one consistent, trustworthy place to fulfil multiple religious obligations throughout the Islamic calendar.

Transparency and Trust: Our Core Commitment

We understand that when it comes to religious obligations like Qurbani and Aqeeqa, trust is everything. Donors need certainty that their sacrifice has actually been performed, that it has been performed correctly according to Shariah guidelines, and that the meat and benefits have reached those who genuinely need it.

This is why Qurbani Easy has been built with transparency as its foundation:

  • Verified Process: Every Qurbani and Aqeeqa booking follows a clearly defined process, from confirmation to execution.
  • Direct Trust Account: All payments go directly into Daan Foundation's official trust account — the same account that has supported our charitable work for years.
  • Dedicated Support: Donors can reach out with questions about their booking at any stage, with a team focused specifically on Qurbani-related services.
  • Community Impact: Meat and resources from Qurbani and Sadqa donations are distributed to underserved families, ensuring that the spiritual act of sacrifice translates into real nutritional and social support for those who need it most.

Secure and Simple Payments

We know that one of the most important parts of any online donation platform is making sure payments are simple, secure, and reach the right place. On Qurbani Easy, donors can now complete their payments easily through QR code or UPI ID, with funds going directly into Daan Foundation's official account. This ensures that every rupee contributed towards Qurbani, Aqeeqa, or Sadqa is accounted for and reaches its intended purpose without delay or confusion.

A Word on Our Shared Mission

Daan Foundation was established with the mission of making charity — in all its forms — accessible, transparent, and impactful. Sadqa, Aqeeqa, and Qurbani are among the most meaningful expressions of that mission because they combine religious devotion with direct, tangible support for people in need.

By building Qurbani Easy as a dedicated platform under the Daan Foundation umbrella, we are not stepping away from our core mission — we are strengthening it. Every Qurbani performed, every Aqeeqa arranged, and every Sadqa given through Qurbani Easy directly supports the same communities that Daan Foundation has always served, while giving donors a more focused, more specialised, and more trustworthy experience for this particular category of giving.

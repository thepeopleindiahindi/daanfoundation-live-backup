Winter Appeal: Warmth for Every Needy

Protecting Lives During Harsh Winters in India

Winter can be extremely difficult for people who do not have proper clothing or shelter. For many poor families, daily survival itself is a challenge — and during cold weather, the lack of warm clothes can become a serious risk to health and life.

Daan Foundation is committed to supporting vulnerable individuals during winter by providing warmth, comfort, and dignity.



Our Winter Support Initiative

Through our Winter Appeal, Daan Foundation works across communities in India to ensure that no one suffers due to lack of warm clothing.

We focus on helping:

Poor and low-income families

Daily wage workers

Elderly individuals

Homeless people

Children living in difficult conditions



What We Provide

To protect people from cold weather, we distribute:

Warm clothes (jackets, sweaters, shawls)

Blankets

Shoes and slippers

Socks and basic winter essentials

These items are sourced through:

Purchase of new materials

Donations from supporters

Collection of reusable old clothes



New & Old — Both Matter

We believe that both new and well-maintained used clothes can make a meaningful difference.

New items are purchased and distributed where needed

Donated clothes are carefully sorted and shared respectfully

Only usable and appropriate items are given to beneficiaries

This approach allows us to reach more people and reduce waste while serving communities effectively.



Stories From the Ground

“A Blanket That Saved the Night”

An elderly man living in difficult conditions shared that receiving a blanket helped him survive cold nights. For him, it was not just a blanket — it was protection.



“Warm Clothes for My Children”

A mother of three said she could not afford winter clothing for her children. Through the winter drive, her children received sweaters and shoes, helping them stay warm and attend school comfortably.



“Small Help, Big Relief”

Daily wage workers often spend long hours outdoors. Receiving warm clothes during winter reduces their hardship and helps them continue working.



Why This Matters

Cold weather affects the poor the most. Without proper clothing:

Health risks increase

Children fall sick

Elderly people suffer the most

Daily life becomes more difficult

Providing winter support is not just charity — it is a basic humanitarian responsibility.



Our Commitment

Daan Foundation is committed to:

Reaching vulnerable people during winter

Distributing warm clothing with dignity

Using resources responsibly

Expanding support to more communities

All our work is carried out only within India, and we do not operate or accept donations from outside India.

Spread Joy This Eid: Supporting Children with Love & Dignity

Daan Foundation’s Eid Gift Initiative (India)

Eid is a time of happiness, gratitude, and sharing. One of the most beautiful traditions of Eid is giving gifts — known as Eidi. It brings smiles to children and strengthens bonds within families and communities.

But for many children across India — especially orphans, children from poor families, and those living in difficult conditions — Eid can pass like any other day, without new clothes, sweets, or gifts.

Daan Foundation is committed to changing that.



Our Eid Gifts Programme

Every year, Daan Foundation runs an Eid support initiative within India to bring happiness to underprivileged children.

Our aim is simple:

👉 No child should feel left out on Eid

We focus on reaching:

Orphan children

Children of widows

Children from low-income families

Vulnerable and needy households



What We Provide

Through this initiative, we try to provide children with meaningful and joyful Eid support, such as:

New clothes

School bags and basic educational items

Toys and small gifts

Sweets and festive treats

These are not just items — they are moments of happiness that children remember.



Why Eid Gifts Matter

For many children, Eid is the most awaited day of the year. Seeing other children celebrate while they cannot often creates silent sadness.

A small gift can:

Bring a genuine smile

Boost confidence

Help children feel included

Reduce the emotional burden on struggling parents

Sometimes, a simple gift becomes a memory that stays for years.



Stories That Inspire

“My Children Smiled This Eid”

Shabana, a widowed mother, shared that she could not afford new clothes for her children. Through Daan Foundation’s support, her children received Eid gifts.She said, “This Eid felt different. My children were happy like others.”



“First Time Receiving Eidi”

A young child from a low-income family received a small Eid gift pack. It was his first time receiving Eidi. His excitement reminded us why this effort matters.



“Small Gifts, Big Happiness”

During one Eid distribution, volunteers saw children hugging their gift packs with joy. These moments reflect the real impact of simple acts of kindness.



How We Distribute

Daan Foundation ensures that Eid gifts are distributed respectfully and directly to beneficiaries within India.

Depending on the situation, support is provided through:

Direct distribution of gift packs

Support to families for purchasing essentials

Community-based distribution drives

We always aim to maintain dignity, transparency, and fairness in distribution.



Our Responsibility Towards Children

Children are a trust (amanah) and caring for them is a shared responsibility.

The teachings of Islam emphasize kindness, love, and care towards children, especially orphans and the vulnerable.

Even a small act — like giving a gift — can build love, compassion, and unity in society.



Our Commitment

Daan Foundation is committed to:

Bringing joy to children during Eid

Supporting vulnerable families within India

Promoting dignity, inclusion, and compassion

Ensuring no child feels forgotten

We operate only within India and focus entirely on local communities and grassroots impact.
